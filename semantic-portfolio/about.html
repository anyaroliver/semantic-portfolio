My approach

I work across strategy, interface design, and front-end development. I care about the details people notice — and the barriers they shouldn’t have to notice at all.

That means building with semantic HTML, designing for keyboard and screen-reader users, and testing assumptions early. Accessibility isn’t a finishing touch; it’s part of the foundation.

What I bring

  • Accessible interface design
  • Semantic HTML and CSS
  • Responsive design systems
  • Content structure and UX writing
  • Collaborative prototyping
